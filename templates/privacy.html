Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. When you use the service, we store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide and improve Spendly — for example, to sign you in, display your expenses, and generate spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our application database. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access, and passwords are never stored in plain text. You may request deletion of your account at any time, after which your associated data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when pages are displayed. We do not share your account or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
